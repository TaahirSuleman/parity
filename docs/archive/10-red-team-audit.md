# Red-Team Audit: Callback · Claim Clock · First Notice
**Status:** v1, Tue 27 Oct 2026 (D0) · **Auditor:** independent; did not write the packs · **Scope:** the 33 files in `projects/{callback,claim-clock,first-notice}/`, checked against `00-constitution.md`, `01-event-pages-rules-and-submission-form.md`, `03-meta-ai-tooling-reference.md` (TR), `06-artefact-standard.md` §4 and the research notes.
**Method:** ≈60 web calls: 30 load-bearing source verifications (§5); 12 whitespace searches (§9); about 8 API-parity, licence and rule checks; 7 fetches failed at the site. Every cost table and impact scenario was recomputed from list prices and stated inputs. Voiceover pacing and X-post lengths were computed by script. Hours were estimated bottom-up per component.
**Limits:** SecurityWeek (6 Oct 2026), Ropes & Gray and Help Net Security (14 Sep 2026) returned site errors, as did npm, two Starling/Cognizant pages and one Varonis URL variant. Where a figure was corroborated elsewhere, that is stated. API behaviour claims marked "(reasoning)" come from public API surfaces I know, not from a fetch in this session.

**Severity scale.** **Critical**: likely to sink the submission or the schedule if not fixed now. **High**: costs rubric points or makes an on-screen claim false. **Medium**: a judge or practitioner could catch it, or it creates avoidable risk. **Low**: polish.
