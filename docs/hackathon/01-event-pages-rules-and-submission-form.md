# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon – Cerebral Valley (public event page)
Source: https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon (scraped 2026-10-26 via browser)

Hosts: Meta & Cerebral Valley
Remote — Oct 26 at 12:00 PM – Nov 4 at 9:00 PM (EST)
"Come and build the future of superintelligence."

Ten days, access to our newest models and $1M in prizes. The Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon is virtual, worldwide and free to enter if selected.

## About the Hackathon
Apply to spend ten days building with Meta's newest models. Use the Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image and SAM 3.1 through Meta Model API, run Meta open weights models, and code faster with Muse Code coding agent.

Bring a problem you know firsthand, or an idea that feels out of reach. Build it with the models, learn from the Meta team and partners in live workshops, and compete for a share in the $1,000,000 cash prize pool across two tracks: one for indie developers and one for startups.

## Partners
- Baseten: $100 in credits and 2M TPM rate limits to select participants to use Meta's open-weights models.
- Modal: $100 in credits to select participants to use Meta's open-weights models through Endpoints in addition to their serverless GPUs, sandboxes, and tools for LLM training.
- Fireworks: $25 in credits to select participants to use Meta's open-weight models along with a workshop from Sinan Ozdemir.

## Participant Tracks
$1,000,000 in total prizes. Every project enters one track.
- Indie developer track: For individual builders and students. Judged on creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works.
- Startup track: For teams and founders building a real product. Judged on technical execution and whether it holds up in production.

## Project Themes
Regardless of the track you choose, we're looking for projects in two main themes:
- Build for Your World. Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before.
- Push the Frontier. Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond. Give a machine a new capability, accelerate a scientific experiment, or unlock a better way to understand and control a physical system. Show us what becomes possible when these models meet the real world.

## Apply Now
Participation is limited to a maximum of 20,000 accepted applicants. Applications evaluated on a rolling basis. Organizers may close the portal once the cap is satisfied.

## Key Dates
- Oct 13, 9:00 AM ET: Applications open (rolling review).
- Oct 25, 11:59 PM ET: Applications Deadline, or earlier if all 20,000 spots are filled.
- Oct 26, 9:00 AM ET: Applicant approval notification sent by this time.
- Oct 26: Hackathon begins with kickoff livestream.
- Oct 27 – Nov 4: Live workshops from Meta and partners, plus office hours on Discord.
- Nov 4: Project submissions due by 9:00 PM ET.
- Nov 4 – Nov 8: Judging.
- Nov 9: Closing ceremony - live finals and winners announced.
*Dates may be subject to change at Meta's sole discretion. Any change will be posted on this page.

## How Applications are Reviewed
Two criteria, weighted equally, using only application-form answers:
- Your project idea: how clear and feasible your proposed project is, and how well it fits one of the two project themes.
- Your technical skill: the technical skill shown in the AI project you describe, including what you built and the techniques you used.
Eligible applications meeting minimum scoring cut-offs accepted automatically, up to the first 20,000. Duplicate applications, false identity information or spam will be disqualified.

## Eligibility
Open worldwide, except where prohibited. Excluded: sanctioned individuals/orgs; those organized or located in Belarus, China, Russia, Ukraine, Syria, Crimea/Donetsk/Luhansk, Cuba, North Korea, Iran. Employees, contractors, interns of Meta and Cerebral Valley, government-affiliated individuals, immediate families and same-household individuals are not eligible.

Link: "View Application Overview and Event Waiver" → /e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver
Official Rules will be published before the Hackathon begins on October 26, 2026 and must be accepted by the selected participants.
Status: Event Closed (applications).

## 4. Hackathon Rules
- **Open source:** Repositories must be public.
- **Team size:** Max four per team. Solo allowed.
- **Demo requirements:** Your demo must only highlight the specific features, code, and functionality that you built during the Hackathon. Judges must be able to clearly identify what was created during the Hackathon. Failure to clearly identify your original contributions will result in immediate disqualification at Judges' sole discretion.
- **New work only:** What you present must have been built during the event. This should be clear in your submissions. Startups may build on existing products or codebases, but must clearly identify what was newly developed during the Hackathon.
- **Banned projects:** Disqualified if they violate legal, ethical, or platform policies, or use code, data, or assets you do not have the rights to.

### Sample Anti-Projects (generic or policy-violating — avoid)
Any project using AI to generate and give medical advice (e.g. AI Mental Health Advisor, skin blemish detector); Basic RAG Applications; Simple Image Analyzers; simple "AI for Education" Chatbots; AI Job Application Screener; AI Nutrition Coach; Personality Analyzers; Sports analyzers or coaches; Anything political or involving war. (Not exhaustive.)

## 7. Submission Process
Submit here by November 4 at 9:00 PM EST: https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit
- One submission per team/startup. Late submissions not considered.
- Each team appoints one Representative to submit.
- Double-check: repo publicly accessible, video link accessible, everyone who worked on the project listed.
- **Three-minute demo video** highlighting the specific features, code, and functionality built during the Hackathon. This video is one of the primary artifacts Judges use, along with your code and live product.

## 8. Judging Process (three stages)
- **Stage One (pass/fail):** baseline viability — Project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably used Meta Model API and/or Meta's open-weight models to build the project in the Hackathon.
- **Stage Two (0–10 scoring on rubric):**

| Category (Weight) | Description |
|---|---|
| **Impact (30%)** | What's the real-world potential here? Who benefits, and how much does it matter? Could this actually become something people use? Does it fit into one of the problem statements listed above? |
| **Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%)** | How creatively did this team use Meta's AI tooling? Did they go beyond a basic integration? Did they surface capabilities that surprised even us? |
| **Technical Depth & Execution (20%)** | Is the engineering sound and thoughtfully refined? Does this feel like something that was thought through with real craft. |
| **Demo (25%)** | Is this a working, impressive demo? Does the product hold up live? Is the demo video genuinely cool to watch? |

- **Stage Three:** Top 5 scoring projects per track move to a final round where final-round Judges watch the video demos live and rank them using the same rubric.
